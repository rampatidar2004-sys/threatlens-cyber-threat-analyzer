Incident summary: ransomware intrusion attributed to the Conti group, April 2022.

On 11th Apr 22 the operators gained entry to the network by logging in through the VPN with valid accounts whose passwords had been harvested earlier.
On 12th Apr 22 the intruders ran Mimikatz on a compromised server to dump credentials from LSASS memory, which gave them domain administrator rights.
Later the same day they used AdFind to enumerate other computers and domain controllers in Active Directory, a form of remote system discovery.
On 12th and 13th Apr 22 the actor moved laterally by copying Cobalt Strike beacons to the C$ admin shares over SMB and launching them remotely with PsExec, which created services on the target hosts.
On 14th Apr 22 the operators used Rclone to upload several hundred gigabytes of stolen files to a MEGA cloud storage account.
On 15th Apr 22 the ransomware was deployed to all servers and workstations and the files were encrypted, and a ransom note was left on every machine.
Backups were also deleted with vssadmin to prevent the victim from recovering the data.
Defenders recommend enforcing multi-factor authentication and monitoring for unusual remote service creation.
